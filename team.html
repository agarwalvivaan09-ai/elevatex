The people behind ElevateX

ElevateX was started by three school students in March 2025. Below are its founders, and the Board of Advisors: members who held key roles as we grew and still guide the current team.

Founders

Vivaan Agarwal

Founder, former CEO & Chairman

Had the idea, made the first phone call, and set up how ElevateX runs.

More

Vivaan started ElevateX with Devyansh and Samridh and led it as CEO. His focus was making it last beyond a handful of events: departments with clear jobs, written-down processes, and people who own their work.

Founder's noteInstagramEmailWebsite

Devyansh Tolasaria

Founder, former COO & Vice Chairman

Built the operational side: who does what, and how work gets done.

More

As COO, Devyansh turned ideas into things that actually happened. He set up how teams coordinate, and made sure work didn't depend on a few people fixing problems at the last minute.

Founder's noteInstagramEmail

Samridh Sanganeria

Founder, former CFO

Set up ElevateX's money side: budgets, allocation and planning.

More

Samridh built the financial system ElevateX runs on, from budgeting to deciding where money goes, so the organisation could take on more without overspending.
