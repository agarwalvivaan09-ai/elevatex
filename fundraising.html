Events

Events & fundraisers

Our events pay for our drives. They're also the one thing the whole organisation works on together: Project Operations runs the event, Finance handles the money, and Marketing gets people through the door.

Five events in eight months

10 May 2025

Tech Tonik

Our first fundraiser, two months after ElevateX started. A neon techno night for more than 100 students. Plenty went wrong, and most of what we now know about running events, we learned that night.

100+ attendeesFirst fundraiser
20 July 2025

Retrograde

A retro red-carpet prom, built on everything Tech Tonik taught us. More than 160 people came, and 160 saplings were planted as part of the event.

160+ attendees160 saplings planted
17 August 2025

Zenith

An online pitch competition in the style of Shark Tank. Entry was free, so anyone with an idea could pitch it. Fourteen teams did.

14 teamsFree entryOnline
9 October 2025

Football Fiesta

Our first sports event, and our first with sponsors. Fourteen teams played, and we found out how much work partnerships and match-day logistics really take.

14 teamsFirst sponsored event
13 December 2025

Euphoria: Winterland

Our biggest event so far. More than 450 people at Orchid Banquets in Kolkata, three months of planning, and a full stage production that nearly didn't happen.

450+ attendees3 months of planning
