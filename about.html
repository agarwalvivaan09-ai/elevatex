About ElevateX

We're a nonprofit run by school students in Kolkata. Since March 2025 we've run welfare drives, taught financial literacy, and organised events big enough to need licences.

Why we started

Most student initiatives follow the same arc. A few people get excited, run two or three events, then exams arrive and it quietly stops. We wanted to build something that keeps going when any one of us gets busy, or leaves.

That meant departments with clear jobs, budgets written down before money is spent, and a way to check whether a drive actually worked. It also meant making ElevateX somewhere people want to show up. The late-night calls and the WhatsApp groups matter at least as much as the structure does.

What we do

Drives. Food and blanket distribution, clean-ups, plantation drives, festival outreach and stray feeding.

Project Unnati. Financial literacy workshops for students, run with NGOs and a partner school.

Events. Fundraisers from techno nights to a 450-person winter event. The money goes back into the drives.

How we're organised

Leadership, a board and five departments

ElevateX is led by a Chairman and Vice Chairman, with a CEO and COO running things day to day. A Board of Advisors, made up of members who held key roles as we grew, advises on the bigger decisions. Every member belongs to one of five departments, each with one or two heads.

All of it runs on our own members-only platform. See the Volunteer Hub

Finance

Sets the budget for every drive and event, approves spending and keeps the receipts.

Marketing

Runs our Instagram, films drives and events, and handles campaigns and partnerships.

HR

Recruits and onboards new members, and checks in on how people are doing.

Project Operations

Plans and runs our fundraisers end to end, from the first idea to the décor.

Drive Execution

Plans drives, handles logistics on the day, and runs Project Unnati sessions.
