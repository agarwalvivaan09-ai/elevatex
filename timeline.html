Timeline

From a phone call to 300 members

What happened between December 2024 and now.

16 Dec 2024

The phone call

Late at night, Vivaan calls Samridh with an idea for a nonprofit. Devyansh joins, and the three of them work on it through their pre-board exams.

8 Mar 2025

ElevateX starts

ElevateX officially begins. The first idea was to support cancer patients; it soon grew into Project Unnati and our welfare drives.

26 Apr 2025

First drive

A clean-up at Victoria Memorial, Kolkata. The first time an idea became something we actually did.

6 May 2025

100 members

Two months in, we pass our first hundred members.

10 May 2025

Tech Tonik

Our first fundraiser, a neon techno night. We learned a lot, mostly the hard way.

20 Jun 2025

Project Unnati launches

Our financial literacy workshops begin.

20 Jul 2025

Retrograde

A retro prom for 160+ people, and a big step up from Tech Tonik in how it was run.

17 Aug 2025

Zenith

Our first online event: student founders pitching ideas, Shark Tank style.

2 Sep 2025

200 members

Membership doubles in four months.

8 Oct 2025

Football Fiesta

Our first sports event, and our first with sponsors.

17 Nov 2025

300 members

Another hundred members in under three months.

13 Dec 2025

Euphoria

450+ people at Orchid Banquets. Our biggest event so far.

15 Jan 2026

Beyond Kolkata

Our first national collaboration, taking ElevateX outside the city.
