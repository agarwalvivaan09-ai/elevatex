How we run ElevateX

The Volunteer Hub

300 volunteers, five departments and a drive almost every weekend don't run on WhatsApp groups. So we built our own platform. Every ElevateX member signs in to the Hub to find drives, sign up, get their credits and follow what the organisation is doing.

The Hub is for members only. Every screenshot on this page uses sample data, so no real member's details are shown.

Drives

From idea to approved drive

Any member can propose a drive. Someone from the core team reviews the plan and approves it, and everyone is notified the moment it goes live.

Volunteering takes one tap. When a drive fills up, members join a waitlist and are told as soon as a spot opens. Every activity carries its own map link or meeting link, instructions and organiser.

Accountability

Every drive gets a score

Before a drive starts, the organiser locks a plan: the target, the budget and the number of volunteers. Afterwards we record what actually happened, and the Hub works out the drive's EDPI score.

Each drive ends with a one-page report, with the score, the money spent per person helped, photos and the full volunteer list. It's ready to print or share with a partner.

Recognition

Credit where it's due

Everyone marked present at a drive, event or meeting gets credits automatically. Nobody can mark their own attendance. Members can see their own record at any time and use it to request a certificate.

Leadership sees the whole picture: credits by month, drive scores side by side, and who's been showing up.

Teamwork

Everyone knows what they own

Heads assign tasks within their department, and leadership can assign to anyone. Each task has an owner, a due date and a progress note, so nothing depends on remembering a WhatsApp message.

Meetings, announcements and polls work the same way: aimed at exactly the right people, whether that's the whole organisation, one department or the board.

Private by design

Most of our members are school students, so privacy came first. Members never see each other's contact details; they see only the core team, who are their point of contact. What each person can do depends on their role, and the database itself enforces those rules, not just the screens.

Built by us

The Hub was designed by ElevateX's founders around how we actually work: our departments, our approval flow and our own Drive Performance Index. It runs at hub.elevate-x.in and works on a laptop or as an app on a phone.
