How we run ElevateX

The Volunteer Hub

300 volunteers, five departments and a drive almost every weekend don't run on WhatsApp groups. So we built our own platform, and every member signs in to it to find drives, sign up and track their credits.

From idea to approved drive

Any member can propose a drive. Someone from the core team reviews the plan and approves it, and everyone is notified the moment it goes live.

Volunteering takes one tap. When a drive fills up, members join a waitlist and hear as soon as a spot opens. Every activity has its own map or meeting link, instructions and organiser.

Every drive gets a score

Before a drive starts, the organiser locks a plan: the target, the budget and the number of volunteers. Afterwards we record what actually happened, and the Hub works out the drive's EDPI score.

Each drive ends with a one-page report showing the score, the money spent per person helped, photos and the volunteer list, ready to print or share with a partner.

Credit where it's due

Everyone marked present at a drive, event or meeting gets credits automatically, and nobody can mark their own attendance. Members can check their record at any time and use it to request a certificate.

Leadership sees the whole picture: credits by month, drive scores side by side, and who has been showing up.

Everyone knows what they own

Heads assign tasks within their department, and leadership can assign to anyone. Each task has an owner, a due date and a progress note, so nothing depends on someone remembering a WhatsApp message.

Meetings, announcements and polls reach exactly the right people, whether that's the whole organisation, one department or the board.

Everything else, in one place

The day-to-day running of ElevateX, from new applications to the monthly report for the board.

Applications

Applications from this website arrive in the Hub. HR reviews them, and accepted applicants get an account in one click.

Announcements

One board for news, with read receipts, so important updates don't get lost in a group chat.

Polls

Quick votes on themes, dates and venues, for everyone or for a single department.

Monthly report

Each month on one page: activities, drive scores and how every department did.

HR and certificates

Feedback, department moves and certificate requests, with each member's credits attached automatically.

Change history

Every change to people, credits and approvals is logged with who made it. Nobody can edit an entry.

Private by design

Most of our members are school students, so privacy came first. Members never see each other's contact details, only the core team's, who are their point of contact. What each person can do depends on their role, and those rules are enforced by the database itself, not just the screens.

Built around how we work

The Hub was designed by ElevateX's founders around our departments, our approval flow and our own Drive Performance Index. It runs at hub.elevate-x.in, on a laptop or as an app on a phone.
