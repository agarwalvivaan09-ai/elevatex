Euphoria: Winterland

Our largest fundraiser. 450+ people, a winter-themed stage, and three months of planning that very nearly fell apart at the end.

450+attendees
3months of planning
2,800venue capacity
1stevent with proper ticketing

How it came together

Euphoria started out as one more fundraiser. As planning went on, it turned into the biggest thing ElevateX had tried, big enough that we set up a separate admin and execution team just to run it.

The venue was Orchid Banquets in Kolkata, which holds more than 2,800 people. At that size everything needs paperwork: licences, permissions, and coordination with the venue and every vendor. It was also the first time we built a proper ticketing system, with early-access passes and controlled entry.

When it nearly didn't happen

In the final stretch, the licensing was uncertain, coordination with the venue got messy, and there were last-minute problems with the DJ. For a while nobody knew whether the night would go ahead. The admin team kept solving one problem at a time, and it did.

On the night

A full stage with lighting and a winter set. DJ Omar played. Belgian Waffles, Wow Momo and Nandus ran food stalls, and there were blackjack and 7-up 7-down tables for anyone who needed a break from the dance floor. Sponsors included Trendoraz and Grid Iron.

Four hundred and fifty people came.
