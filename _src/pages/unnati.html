Project Unnati

Money basics, taught early

Project Unnati is our financial literacy programme. Since June 2025 we've run workshops on saving, budgeting and basic investing for students who don't get taught any of it at school.

What a session looks like

Sessions are interactive and built on everyday examples, not textbook definitions. We cover saving, budgeting, and how to make basic decisions about money, the things most people only figure out once they've already made the mistakes.

Workshops are run by our Drive Execution team.

Who we work with

Most sessions are run with local NGOs, who help us reach students with the least access to this kind of teaching.

We've also started working with a partner school, our first step towards teaching this inside a classroom.
