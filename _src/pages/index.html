How it started

Three students and a late-night phone call

On the night of 16 December 2024, Vivaan Agarwal called Samridh Sanganeria with an idea for a nonprofit. With Devyansh Tolasaria, they worked on it through their pre-board exams, and ElevateX officially started on 8 March 2025.

Seven weeks later they ran their first drive, a clean-up at Victoria Memorial. By November there were 300 members. In December, 450 people came to Euphoria.
