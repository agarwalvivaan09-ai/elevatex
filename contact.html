What being a member looks like

You join one department, volunteer at drives and help run events. Tasks come from your department head, and you earn credits every time you're marked present at a drive, event or meeting.

  • Finance · budgets for drives and events, receipts
  • Marketing · Instagram, filming, campaigns
  • HR · recruiting, onboarding, looking after members
  • Project Operations · running our fundraisers
  • Drive Execution · planning drives and Project Unnati
