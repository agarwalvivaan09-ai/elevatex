Join ElevateX

ElevateX is for school students who'd rather run things than just turn up. Fill in a short form and someone from our HR team will get in touch.

Open the application form Already a member? Log in

What being a member looks like

You join one department, volunteer at drives and help run events. Tasks come from your department head, and the hours you volunteer are verified and recorded on our Volunteer Hub.

Finance

Budgets for drives and events, approving spending, keeping receipts.

Marketing

Instagram, filming drives and events, campaigns and partnerships.

HR

Recruiting and onboarding members, and checking in on how people are doing.

Project Operations

Running our fundraisers, from the first idea to the décor.

Drive Execution

Planning drives, logistics on the day, and Project Unnati sessions.

Questions?
